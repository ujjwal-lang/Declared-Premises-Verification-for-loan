What this prototype is arguing

  • Capture happens inside the lender's app, so evidence never crosses WhatsApp and never loses its metadata.
  • Every quality problem is caught on the device, seconds after it happens, not days later by a reviewer.
  • Quality failures explain themselves. Integrity failures deliberately do not.
  • No screen in this flow rejects anyone. Failures move to another rail.
  • The decision is a score with reasons, handed to credit policy. It is not an approval.
